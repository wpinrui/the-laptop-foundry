Review Northfold Tessera 14 Notebook

14/09/2006
The test unit

The Northfold Tessera 14 is a business notebook built around the Intel Core 2 Duo T7600 and GMA 950 (chipset).

The chassis is made of magnesium alloy and plastic and measures 334 by 243 mm at 33.5 mm thick. It weighs 2.28 kg.

Specifications

Case and connectivity

The case feels built to last. The base survived our 76 cm drop test, and the magnesium alloy lid flexes only 1.8 mm under a firm press.

The left side has USB 2.0, USB 2.0, VGA; the right side has FireWire, PC Card, audio; the rear side has DC in. Charging only works from the rear side.

Wireless duties fall to 802.11a/b/g.

Northfold Aero 14 review: the quiet overachiever

A 120 Hz 2.5K panel, Panther Lake and 13 hours on a charge in 1.24 kg. Northfold builds the ultrabook to beat this year.

Northfold pitches the Aero 14 as a premium ultrabook, and on paper the Intel Core Ultra X9 388H and Arc B390 fit that brief.

Next to the competition, a few things impress: long battery life, holds its performance under sustained load, sharp high-resolution display, light for its class.

It is not flawless. Our list of complaints: shallow keyboard.

Priced at $1,299, it makes the competition look expensive.
