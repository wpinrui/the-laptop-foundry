## A320neo

The aircraft on the lounge apron are the A320neo from [wpinrui/tower-golf-oscar](https://github.com/wpinrui/tower-golf-oscar/tree/main/scripts/model) (owned by the project owner), ported from GDScript to three.js geometry without changing its dimensions, profiles or paint values.

## Built for this project

Everything else was built for The Laptop Foundry and falls under the repository's own licence. That includes the geometry of the benches, tables, rooms, carriage, cabin, deck, railings, cyclorama, trees and their leaf atlas, the gaming chair, the banker's lamp, and the procedural textures (leaves, posters, monitor image, book spines, moquette, awning, blanket, apron concrete, window interiors).
