All SFX and ambience are CC0 (no attribution required; credit given as courtesy). Music: office, map and menu are CC0 (HoliznaCC0); the other four are CC BY 4.0 and the attribution lines below MUST be shown in the game credits.

Freesound files were taken from the site's HQ preview MP3 of each sound (same licence as the sound). All files re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis 44.1 kHz by a build script kept outside the repo (SFX mono, peak -3 dBFS; ambience stereo seamless loops, -28 to -34 LUFS; music stereo, -16 LUFS).
