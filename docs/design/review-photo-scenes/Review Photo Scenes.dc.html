Review photo scenes
SpaceUnits are mm, y up. The laptop sits as in viewer/space.ts: base centred on the origin, bottom at y 0, facing +z, user's left on -x.
Scalek = width / 340. Scaled sets are multiplied by k, so every laptop fills the photo alike. Real sets stay in true mm, so a small laptop looks small on the table.
Anglesaz 0 is straight in front, positive toward the laptop's right. el is up from level. Lid is degrees open, 0 shut.
FramingThe camera sits on the shot's direction. Aim and distance are solved so the named bounds fill the given share of the 16:9 frame. No randomness anywhere.
RendersRGB out, no tone mapping, PCF shadows at 2048, RoomEnvironment reflections at each set's intensity. Code: review-scenes.js, buildShot(scene, shot, era, size).
